About Me

I am currently completing my Master’s degree in Architecture, with the submission of my dissertation expected in July 2026. I consider myself a dedicated, responsible, and enthusiastic person, always eager to learn new skills and embrace new challenges. I value communication and teamwork, and I enjoy collaborating in creative and dynamic environments.

I am passionate about discovering new places and seeking opportunities that allow me to experience international cultures and adventures. Outside of architecture, I spend much of my free time exploring creative activities such as drawing, painting, reading, writing, and crochet, as well as engaging in sports and outdoor activities including volleyball, pilates, dance, hiking, and snorkeling.
